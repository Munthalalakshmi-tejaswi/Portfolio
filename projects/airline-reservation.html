Airline Reservation System

SQL · MySQL

Scalable Airline Reservation & Flight Management System

A SQL-based project that models core airline operations, from airport and aircraft records to flight scheduling, passenger bookings, seat assignment, and payments. Stored procedures and triggers automate booking rules, while SQL queries produce operational and revenue reports.

MySQLRelational databaseStored proceduresTriggers

Database structure

Seven related entities model the main reservation lifecycle.

Airports

Name, city, country, and airport code.

Aircraft & seats

Aircraft models, capacity, seat numbers, and cabin class.

Flights

Flight numbers, routes, schedules, assigned aircraft, and status.

Passengers

Passenger identity and contact records.

Bookings

Passenger and flight links, assigned seats, booking dates, and status.

Payments

Booking charges, payment method, and payment status.

Booking and payment automation

Automatic seat allocation

AllocateSeat assigns an available seat for a passenger and flight. CheckAvailableSeats lists seats still open on a flight.

Booking validation

A double-booking prevention trigger protects seat assignments from being duplicated for a flight.

Payment status

UpdatePaymentStatus records payment amount, method, and result for a booking.

Failed-payment handling

A trigger automatically cancels a booking when its payment fails.

Revenue & occupancy

Summarize successful payments and flight seat usage.

-- Revenue by flight from successful payments
SELECT f.flight_number, SUM(pay.amount) AS total_revenue
FROM Flights f
JOIN Bookings b ON f.flight_id = b.flight_id
JOIN Payments pay ON b.booking_id = pay.booking_id
WHERE pay.payment_status = 'Success'
GROUP BY f.flight_number;
-- Check available seats for a flight
CALL CheckAvailableSeats(1);
